ABOUT M.J ACADEMY

Education With Purpose

M.J Academy is committed to providing quality Islamic and academic education in a safe, disciplined and positive environment.

Our Vision

To build a generation that is strong in Deen, education, character and leadership, empowering children, youth and women with authentic Islamic knowledge and quality education.

Our Mission

To provide accessible and quality Islamic education, develop strong Aqeedah, Akhlaaq and Adab, and combine Deeni education with modern academic learning.

Our Motto

Learn Deen • Build Character • Educate the Future
