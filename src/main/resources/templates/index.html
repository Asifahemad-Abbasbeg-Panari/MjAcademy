ABOUT M.J. ACADEMY

Empowering Minds. Nurturing Character.

M.J. Academy is an educational initiative established with a clear purpose — to create a learning environment where knowledge, Islamic values, discipline, character and modern education come together.

About M.J. Academy

Our journey began in December 2024 with just 5 students and a vision to make meaningful education accessible to the community. Since then, M.J. Academy has continued to grow through dedication, teamwork and the trust of students, parents and well-wishers.

We believe education is not limited to textbooks or examinations. True education develops a person's knowledge, character, confidence, responsibility and ability to contribute positively to society.

Our Foundation

M.J. Academy was founded in December 2024 by Waheedabanu, with Suhel Mulla as Co-Founder.

The founders envisioned an institution that would provide learners with opportunities to acquire Islamic knowledge, Quranic understanding, academic education, life skills and leadership qualities in a disciplined and supportive environment.

Our Organisation

M.J. Academy operates under Moulana Jalaluddin Welfare Society, a registered society established to undertake educational and welfare-oriented activities.

Society Registration Date: 18 December 2025

Registration Framework: Karnataka Societies Registration Act, 1960

The organisation aims to conduct educational and community-oriented initiatives in a structured, responsible and transparent manner.

OUR PURPOSE

Vision & Mission

Building knowledge, character and values for a better future.

Our Vision

“To create a generation rooted in values, enriched with knowledge and prepared to lead with responsibility.”

Our vision is to develop learners who are:

Knowledgeable • Ethical • Disciplined • Confident • Responsible • Spiritually Connected

We aspire to build an educational environment where Islamic values and modern educational development complement each other.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide accessible and meaningful education through:

  • Quality Islamic education
  • Quran and Tajweed learning
  • Deeniyat and Islamic studies
  • Academic support and tuition
  • Women's and girls' education
  • Character and moral development
  • Leadership and talent development
  • Online and offline learning opportunities
  • Student-focused teaching and mentoring

We aim to help every learner discover their potential and use their knowledge for the benefit of themselves, their families and society.
